1. Anonymous Profile & Local Data

Tic-Tac-Toe Ultimate does not require mandatory registration or personal accounts. Online multiplayer rooms operate using temporary anonymous sessions, and local settings are saved on your device.

2. How to Delete Your Data

  • Clear Local Data: Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings immediately deletes all locally stored preferences, player names, and match history.
  • Delete Online Session Data / Request Data Removal: Temporary room data (rooms/{roomCode}) is deleted automatically when you close or leave a room. If you wish to request complete removal of any anonymous cloud identifier or support logs, please use Settings → Contact Us in the app or email us directly at slaboratory.support@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 business days.
