Privacy Policy — Tic-Tac-Toe Ultimate

Effective Date: November 7, 2026
Developer: sLaboratory
Application ID: com.slaboratory.tictactoeultimate
Contact Email: slaboratory.support@gmail.com

1. Overview

sLaboratory ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Tic-Tac-Toe Ultimate collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data We Collect

  • Local Game Preferences: Player names, selected neon colors, symbols, sound themes, match settings, and animation options are stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences.
  • Online Multiplayer Rooms: When you create or join an Online Room by room code, we use anonymous Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to temporarily synchronize room state, player display names, moves, turn timers, and the 2-line in-room chat during the active match.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): Unless you purchase the one-time "Remove Ads" option, the app displays a top banner ad and occasional interstitial ads between matches (at most once every 5 minutes). Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID), coarse location, and ad interaction metrics in accordance with your GDPR/UMP consent preferences.
  • In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Google Play Billing): If you purchase "Remove Ads", transaction validation is handled securely by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat using anonymous purchase identifiers.
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect anonymized usage statistics and crash stack traces to improve stability.

3. Managing Ad Consent (GDPR / UMP)

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and other applicable regions can review or change their advertising consent at any time inside the app via Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Temporary online room data is automatically cleaned up after matches conclude. To request deletion of any associated anonymous online identifier or data, please see our Account & Data Deletion page or contact us directly at slaboratory.support@gmail.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at slaboratory.support@gmail.com.
